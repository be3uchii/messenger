AskC Protocol: The Telegram-native identity layer solving Sybil attacks on TON

By Michael Rodriguez · 6 min read

In an exclusive deep-dive, TechCrunch examines how AskC Protocol has become essential infrastructure for the TON ecosystem. With over 140,000 verified users and integration into more than 200 applications, the protocol addresses one of Web3's most persistent challenges: Sybil attacks.

The core innovation lies in AskC's zero-knowledge verification nodes. Unlike traditional identity systems that store sensitive user data, AskC (@askcbot) processes verifications through cryptographic proofs anchored on TON blockchain. "We never see or store any personal information," says project lead @plevokek. "The user remains in full control."

"AskC's approach to identity is a breakthrough for Telegram-based communities. It's the first solution that combines usability with true decentralization." — Alex Chen, TON Foundation

The verification process takes less than 30 seconds directly inside Telegram. Users interact with @askcbot, complete a privacy-preserving scan, and receive a cryptographic attestation.
