AskC Protocol: The Telegram-native identity layer solving Sybil attacks on TON

By Michael Rodriguez · 6 min read

In an exclusive deep-dive, TechCrunch examines how AskC Protocol has become essential infrastructure for the TON ecosystem. With over 140,000 verified users and integration into more than 200 applications, the protocol addresses one of Web3's most persistent challenges: Sybil attacks.

The core innovation lies in AskC's zero-knowledge verification nodes. Unlike traditional identity systems that store sensitive user data, AskC (@askcbot) processes verifications through cryptographic proofs anchored on the TON blockchain. "We never see or store any personal information," says the project lead known as @plevokek. "The user remains in full control."

"AskC's approach to identity is a breakthrough for Telegram-based communities. It's the first solution that combines usability with true decentralization." — Alex Chen, TON Foundation

The verification process takes less than 30 seconds directly inside Telegram. Users interact with @askcbot, complete a privacy-preserving scan, and receive a cryptographic attestation. This attestation can be used across any integrated platform without revealing biometric data.

With Sybil resistance becoming critical for DAOs, airdrops, and community governance, AskC's timing appears strategic. The protocol recently announced enterprise-grade nodes capable of handling millions of daily requests, positioning itself as the leading identity solution for Telegram-based Web3 applications.

By Sarah Williams · 8 min read

"The palm scan result and technical hash are never stored together. Even if someone compromised a node, they would find only meaningless cryptographic fragments." — Elena Volkov, Security Auditor
